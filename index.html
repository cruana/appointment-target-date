ℹ️ How to use this tool · Tips & glossary

How to use it

  1. Pick the scenario: a new or existing request, a cancellation/no-show, or a crisis.
  2. For a request, choose the appointment category. The tool shows the rule and asks only for the dates that category needs.
  3. Enter the dates. The tool calculates the Due-by Date — the standard access deadline.
  4. Enter the first offered appointment date. The tool checks it against the Due-by Date and tells you whether access is met or escalation is needed.

Terms

Target Due Date
When the request started (referral created, booked at point of service, or the patient's first call), or a date the provider specified. This is the date you enter in HealthConnect. Here it's informational — not the date compliance is judged against.
Due-by Date
The access deadline set by California timely-access standards (SB855/SB221), calculated from the Target Due Date using the KP Standard window. Compliance is judged against this date.
EPN / In-Network
Outside in-network partners (Rula, Lucet, others). An EPN offer is treated like an internal offer. A declined EPN offer counts as compliant — timely access was offered.
RLD
Used when the patient was offered a timely appointment but requested a later date. Note "RLD" in the appointment notes; the later date still counts as compliant.
Warm transfer
Connecting the patient directly to the home-clinic On Duty clinician (used for crisis and urgent). No target date is calculated.

🛑 STOP — Do not book. Warm transfer.

Warm transfer the patient to the home-clinic On Duty clinician (same destination as Urgent). If the On Duty clinician can't be reached, use the Access & Crisis Line as backup.

Criteria: suicidal or homicidal risk, hallucinations, or domestic violence within 48 hrs.
